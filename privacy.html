UniClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, UniClip may process video files, audio, transcripts, subtitles, clip metadata, and information needed to connect third-party services.

2. TikTok Authorization

If TikTok integration is enabled, you may be redirected to TikTok to authorize access. UniClip may receive authorization tokens and use them to perform actions you approve. TikTok handles information according to its own privacy policy.

3. Local Storage

Some files and settings may be stored on your device. Actual storage depends on the version and configuration you use. Review the application code to understand precisely what is stored.

4. Sharing

Content you choose to publish is sent to the relevant platform. UniClip should not send your content to other services unless required for a feature you use or you authorize it.

5. Security and Retention

Keep credentials and authorization tokens private. Data retention depends on where files and tokens are stored and when you delete them. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You can revoke TikTok authorization through the relevant account settings and delete local application files. For questions or data requests, contact the project maintainer through the UniClip GitHub repository.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when UniClip changes.
